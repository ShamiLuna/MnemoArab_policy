MnemoArab Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026
Last updated: October 8, 2026
Developer: Ashifshamina

MnemoArab is a Tamil–Arabic vocabulary learning app that uses sound-alike mnemonics, flashcards, quizzes, matching activities, and scheduled reviews. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

MnemoArab does not require a user account. Learning progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. The app displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob, which processes certain device and usage information. Pronunciation uses your device's text-to-speech services.

1. Information stored on your device

MnemoArab stores information locally to provide its learning features and remember your preferences. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Learning progress and mastered or learned words.
  • Flashcard and spaced-repetition review information.
  • Quiz results, experience points, and learning streaks.
  • Language, translation-display, audio, and other preferences.

We do not operate a developer-controlled server that receives your learning progress, and the app does not provide an account or developer-operated cloud synchronization service.

Device or operating-system backup services may back up or restore app data according to your device settings and the app's backup configuration.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

MnemoArab uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner ads. Google and advertising partners may process information to deliver advertisements, measure their performance, and prevent fraud and abuse.

Google's SDK documentation identifies automatically collected and shared information that includes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad interactions.
  • Diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable identifiers.

The information processed can depend on the SDK version, advertising configuration, device settings, applicable law, and your privacy choices. Keeping learning progress on your device does not prevent advertising services from processing their own data.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps and sites
  • How Google uses AdMob data

3. Advertising consent and choices

Where required, the app presents an advertising privacy or consent message using Google's User Messaging Platform. Where a privacy-options entry point is required and available, you can revisit your choices through the app's Privacy Options.

You can also manage advertising identifiers and related ad controls in your device settings. Available controls vary by operating-system version and region.

Non-personalized or limited advertising may still involve data processing for purposes such as ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

4. Text-to-speech pronunciation

MnemoArab uses your device's text-to-speech engine to pronounce words and example sentences. Text selected for pronunciation is passed to that engine.

Some installed voices work on-device, while others may require network access or processing by the text-to-speech provider. This depends on your engine, installed voices, and device settings. The provider's privacy policy applies to any provider-side processing.

Pronunciation does not require MnemoArab to record your voice. The app does not use microphone recordings for its described text-to-speech features.

5. Internet access and offline use

Core learning content and locally saved progress are designed to work offline. Internet access is used for advertising and may be needed by certain text-to-speech voices or when you open external links.

An unavailable advertisement does not require you to create an account or upload your learning progress.

6. Permissions

The described app features do not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone recordings, or precise device location.

The app and its advertising SDK may use internet access, network-related capabilities, and advertising identifiers. Google may estimate approximate location from an IP address without requesting precise location permission.

7. Information you send when contacting us

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and attached screenshots. We use it to respond to your request and investigate reported problems.

Please do not send passwords, financial details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

8. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your locally stored learning progress or upload it to our own server. Advertising information is processed by Google and its advertising partners as described above.

Text-to-speech providers and email providers may process information when you use those services. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

9. Retention and deletion

Local learning data is retained until it is removed through an available in-app reset function, your device's app-storage controls, or uninstallation. Operating-system backups may retain or restore data according to the provider's settings and policies.

Clearing MnemoArab's local data does not automatically delete information already processed by Google or other third-party providers. Their retention and deletion policies apply to their records.

Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of correspondence, subject to those obligations.

MnemoArab does not offer user accounts, so there is no MnemoArab account to delete.

10. Children and young users

If you are a parent or guardian with concerns about a child's information, please contact us using the details below. We will review the concern and take appropriate action.

Any audience and advertising restrictions applicable to the app must also be reflected in its store listing and technical configuration.

11. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the app's design to protect information. Local data is stored within the app's device storage and is subject to your device's security protections.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Local app preferences are not a suitable place for sensitive information such as passwords or payment details.

12. International processing

Advertising, text-to-speech, and other service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy policies describe applicable processing practices and safeguards.

13. External links

If you open an external website, that website's privacy policy applies. This includes the hosting provider for this privacy policy page.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice.
